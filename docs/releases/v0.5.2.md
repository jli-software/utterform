# Utterform 0.5.2 — Flow

The same broad ribbon, with a softer finish.

The monochrome pixel ribbon now settles gently over about 1.8 seconds after completion instead of stopping abruptly. Transitions through processing stay smooth, while individual particles flow independently in response to speech. The broad shape, fine filaments and compact layout from 0.5.1 remain intact in light and dark appearance.

Pause still freezes the ribbon immediately. Reduced-motion preferences keep it still, and hidden windows stop decorative animation work.

This update changes the visual motion only. Recording, pause/resume, transcription, prompts, vocabulary, shortcuts, sounds and output delivery are unchanged. Existing settings, keys, history and local models remain available after updating with the normal installer.

## Downloads

Get [Utterform 0.5.2](https://github.com/jli-software/utterform/releases/tag/v0.5.2). Stable filenames are unchanged:

- Windows: `utterform-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` or portable `utterform-windows-x86_64.exe`.
- Linux: `install-linux.sh` and `utterform-linux-x86_64-system.tar.gz`.
- macOS Apple Silicon: `utterform-macos-aarch64.dmg` or `utterform-macos-aarch64.app.zip`.

Windows builds are unsigned; macOS bundles are ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Verify downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
