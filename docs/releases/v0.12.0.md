# Utterform 0.12.0 — The right action in every app

*Stage of the road to 1.0, merged into `main`; not released on its own. It ships with 1.0.*

Until now Utterform used one action everywhere, the one chosen in its window. Dictating a
mail meant switching to *Email* first, and back to *Plain* before the next command in the
terminal. Now an app can have an action of its own.

**Per app.** Under **Settings → Context → Per app**, give Thunderbird *Email* and the
terminal *Plain*. Press the dictation key in Thunderbird and the text arrives as an email;
press it in the terminal and it arrives word for word. In every other app the action in
the window applies, as before. The list starts empty, so nothing changes until you add an
app.

**Adding an app** takes one click: **+ App** lists the apps you dictated into lately — so
dictate into an app once, and it is there. An app that has not come up yet can be typed by
name. Each app has its action menu next to it and × to remove it.

**Never a surprise.** When an app's action takes over, a short notification says so
("Utterform · Email — for Thunderbird"), and the window shows the action and the app while
it records. A recording started from Utterform's own window follows the window, and live
dictation stays word for word.

**Where it works.** Linux under Hyprland (Omarchy) and Sway, X11 with `xprop`, Windows and
macOS. GNOME and KDE on Wayland do not tell other applications which window is focused;
there **Per app** says so and stays off.

**Privacy.** The names of the apps you dictated into are kept in `settings.json` on this
device, at most eight. The diagnostic log notes that an app was found and which action
ran, never which app.
