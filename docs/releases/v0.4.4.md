# Utterform 0.4.4 — A gap is not a failure, and a dot on the tray

Testing 0.4.3 on Windows turned up three things in an afternoon: a recording that could not be finished after a docking station was re-plugged, a start click that was easy to miss, and no way to see that recording had begun at all. This release answers all three.

## "Microphone stream failed: A buffer underrun or overrun occurred"

After plugging a dock and microphone back in, every recording on Windows ended with this message and no text. Nothing was wrong with the microphone: Windows flags a short gap in a capture stream that keeps running, and the audio library passes that on as an *underrun or overrun*. It is a note that a few milliseconds went missing, not a report that the stream ended. Utterform treated it as fatal and discarded the recording.

A gap is now counted and logged, and the recording carries on. Only an error that means the device is gone ends it. The same applies on macOS, which reports a processor overload the same way, and on Linux.

## A start click you can hear

The click that confirms recording is now about 6 dB louder than the stop click and audible for longer. It was calibrated for quiet headphones; through a laptop speaker in a room with other noise it could go unnoticed, and the one cue you are waiting for has to be the one you cannot miss. It is still a click, not an alarm. The stop click and the completion chime are unchanged.

## The tray shows recording

While a recording runs, the tray icon carries a red dot in its lower right corner, and on Windows and macOS the tooltip says *Utterform — recording*. It returns to normal when the recording finishes, is cancelled, or hits the ten-minute limit.

The icon is drawn from the app icon at runtime rather than shipped as a second file, so it cannot fall out of step. On Linux it goes through Utterform's own StatusNotifierItem; on Windows and macOS through the native tray.

**On Windows 11 the tray hides new icons behind the overflow arrow.** Drag Utterform's icon onto the taskbar corner once, or the dot is there and you will not see it. Omarchy's panel shows it straight away.

The sounds remain the primary confirmation; the dot is for the moment a speaker is asleep, muted or unplugged.

## Upgrading

Nothing to configure. The recording-feedback setting still governs the sounds; the tray icon changes regardless of it.
