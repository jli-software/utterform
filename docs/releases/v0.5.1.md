# Utterform 0.5.1 — Signal ribbon

A broader signal. More detail. The same compact window.

The recording view now carries a wide monochrome ribbon of fine pixels, interwoven filaments and scattered edge particles. It fills the available recording width instead of shrinking into a narrow band in compact windows, in both light and dark appearance.

Movement follows the real microphone envelope. Pause freezes the ribbon, reduced-motion preferences keep it still, and animation stops while the window is hidden.

Recording, pause/resume, transcription, prompts, vocabulary, shortcuts, sounds and output delivery are unchanged. Existing settings, keys, history and local models remain available after updating with the normal installer.

## Downloads

Get [Utterform 0.5.1](https://github.com/jli-software/utterform/releases/tag/v0.5.1). Stable filenames are unchanged:

- Windows: `utterform-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` or portable `utterform-windows-x86_64.exe`.
- Linux: `install-linux.sh` and `utterform-linux-x86_64-system.tar.gz`.
- macOS Apple Silicon: `utterform-macos-aarch64.dmg` or `utterform-macos-aarch64.app.zip`.

Windows builds are unsigned; macOS bundles are ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Verify downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
