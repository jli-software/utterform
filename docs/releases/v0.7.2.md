# Utterform 0.7.2 — the shortcut you press, and a start you do not have to

Two things you used to do by hand. The dictation key is now recorded by pressing it
instead of spelled out in a text box, and Utterform can start with your session and
wait in the tray. Everything else is unchanged.

## Recording the dictation key

**Settings → Output → Dictation key** no longer asks you to write `Ctrl+Alt+D`
correctly. Click the field, press the combination you want, and that is the
shortcut. The key is read by its place on the keyboard, so the same physical key
gives the same shortcut on a German and on an American layout. `Escape` keeps the
one you had and leaves Settings open; a key without `Ctrl`, `Alt`, `Shift` or
`Super`/`Cmd` is refused where you pressed it, because reserving a bare key
system-wide would stop it typing in every other application. On a Mac the key it
stores as `Super` is shown as `Cmd`.

While the field is listening, the shortcut Utterform already holds is handed back to
the system for those few seconds — otherwise the combination you are trying to press
would start a recording instead of being read — and taken again the moment the
reading ends, whether you set a new key, pressed Escape, or clicked away. As before,
the new key only becomes permanent with **Save settings**, and one another
application already holds is reported in the field rather than silently lost.

Wayland is unchanged: the compositor owns the keyboard there, so Omarchy keeps its
`bind = SUPER, D, exec, utterform --toggle` line and sees no recorder.

## Starting with your session

**Settings → General → Startup** has a switch: *Start Utterform when I sign in*. It
starts hidden in the system tray — no window, no focus taken from whatever you signed
in to do — and is off until you turn it on.

- **Windows:** an entry for your user, no administrator rights.
- **macOS:** a LaunchAgent for your user. Test it with Utterform in `/Applications`,
  not from the mounted disk image.
- **Linux:** an XDG autostart entry in `~/.config/autostart`, no systemd service.
- **Wayland/Omarchy:** autostart keeps Utterform ready in the tray; dictation is
  still started by your compositor binding.

The switch shows what your system actually says rather than a setting of ours — there
is no autostart field in `settings.json` to disagree with it. It is read when Settings
opens, written only by **Save settings**, only when you changed it, and read back
afterwards to confirm. Cancel changes nothing on your system.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. On Linux the autostart entry points at the installed executable path,
which does not change when you reinstall or update, so a switch you turned on stays
on. The internal argument the entry carries is not a command to use yourself.
